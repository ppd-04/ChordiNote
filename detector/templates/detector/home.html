How It Works

Four simple steps from audio to chords

1

Upload Your Audio

Drop any .wav or .mp3 file — a song, a guitar recording, or even your voice humming a melody.

2

Signal Decomposition

The audio is split into tiny windows. Each window is analyzed using FFT to extract frequency peaks.

3

Note & Chroma Mapping

Frequencies are mapped to the 12 musical pitch classes (C, C#, D... B) creating a chroma profile.

4

Chord Matching

The chroma profile is compared against known chord templates to identify the most likely chord.
