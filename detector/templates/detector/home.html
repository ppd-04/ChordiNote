How It Works

Signal Analysis

We use FFT and autocorrelation to break down audio into its frequency components.

Note Detection

Chord Recognition

Two Detection Methods

Autocorrelation

Time-Domain Method

  • Works directly with the raw audio waveform
  • Finds how the signal repeats itself
  • Great for single-note detection
  • Less effective with complex harmonies

FFT Spectrum

Frequency-Domain Method

  • Transforms audio into frequency components
  • Reveals all frequencies present at once
  • Better for chord detection (multiple notes)
  • Uses Chroma features for robust matching
